CIMA EquipmentHUB

Explore CIMA's analytical infrastructure, including the catalogue of shared equipment, technician profiles, available analytical services, and technical documentation.

What is the EquipmentHUB for?

EquipmentHUB provides a centralized catalogue of all shared equipment available at CIMA. Each equipment page brings together the information needed to understand and use the equipment correctly, including descriptions, technical specifications, applications, methodologies, operating protocols, and supporting documentation.

What can I do in the EquipmentHUB?

EquipmentHUB allows users to check equipment availability and make reservations, access manuals and technical information about analytical services, check technician availability, and explore the applications and specifications of each piece of equipment.

Shared Equipment

The shared-use equipment presented on this page consists exclusively of equipment acquired by the Centre through its own funding and is intended for shared use by all its members. To use equipment acquired through individual members' projects that have since been completed, and whose maintenance is currently ensured by the Centre, it is necessary to contact the respective equipment custodian directly. To consult the list of these items of equipment, click Additional Common Equipment List

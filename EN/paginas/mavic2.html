DJI Mavic 2 Pro Drone

DJI Mavic 2 drone, designed for aerial image and video acquisition in research, environmental monitoring, surveying, and documentation of areas of interest. The equipment combines an autonomous and stabilized flight system with a high-resolution camera mounted on a three-axis gimbal, enabling the acquisition of stable and detailed aerial imagery.

The system allows for programmed flights and surveying operations across different types of terrain and environments, making it suitable for monitoring coastal areas, habitats, water bodies, vegetation, and infrastructure, as well as for visual documentation and the acquisition of georeferenced data when used in conjunction with GNSS systems and appropriate processing software.

Main Applications
  • Environmental and coastal monitorization;
  • Study area survey and documentation;
  • Aerial image acquisition;
  • Vegetation and habitat monitoring;
  • Area and infrastructure visual inspection;
  • Map and orthophotography production by overlaping images;
  • Environmental alteration timeline monitoring;
Main Advantages:
  • High mobility and ease of transport;
  • Camera stabilized by a 3-axis gimbal;
  • High resolution image and video acquisition;
  • Stable flight system and pilot assistance functionalities;
  • Obstacle detection sensors, depending on flight direction;
  • Possibility of automated flights;
  • Adequate for extensive area surveying;
  • Image use for photogrametry and cartography;
  • GNSS coordenates integration for support and georeferencing

VWR LAG 314i analytical balance, with a maximum capacity of 310 g and a readability of 0.1 mg, designed for high-precision weighing in laboratory and research applications. The equipment features automatic internal calibration and a large graphic display, providing intuitive operation and suitability for a wide range of weighing procedures.

The LAG 314i includes checkweighing, totalization, and parts counting functions, as well as different filtering levels and measurement units. The system also supports data recording in accordance with GLP principles, including date, time, and serial number information, making it suitable for the preparation of reagents, solutions, and samples requiring high precision.

Main Applications
  • Sample weighting;
  • Reagent weighting;
  • Mass Variations;

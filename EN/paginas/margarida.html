Margarida Ramires

Research Support Technician Office 2.6 (Inside of Laboratory 2.8) Building 7 Contact: mramires@ualg.pt

Margarida da Conceição Pereira Ramires is a Technician of Centro de Investigação Marinha e Ambiental (CIMA), where she gives technical support to a variety of laboratories and research projects. With more than two decades of sedimentology experience, field work and laboratory management, Margarida plays an essential role in the operation and maintenance os scientific equipments, in performing laboratory analysis and support in sample-collecting campaigns.

Margarida is responsable for the operation and technical support of the Malvern Mastersizer 3000 equipment, colaborating equally in sedimentology analysis, sample preparation and field-data acquisition. Beyond the analytical component, she assures the management of chemical residues, equipment maintenance, consumables management and logistic support to CIMA's research activities.

Specialization Areas:

  • Granulometric analysis by laser difraction;
  • Sedimentology characterization;
  • Sample processing and preparation;
  • Management and maintenance of scientific equipment;
  • Laboratory residue managemnet;
  • Technical support in field campaigns;
  • Topographic surveys;
  • Drone data acquisition support;
  • Scientific data processing and organization;
Available services:

Researchers can contact Margarida for:

  • Granulometric analysis of sediments using Malvern Mastersizer 3000;
  • Preparation and treatment of sedimentological samples;
  • Support in selecting the particle size analysis methodology;
  • Technical support in sediment sampling campaigns;
  • Operation of field equipment;
  • Topographic surveys;
  • Support in the use of drones for scientific work;
  • Laboratory waste management;
  • Equipment maintenance and operation;
  • Laboratory consumables management.

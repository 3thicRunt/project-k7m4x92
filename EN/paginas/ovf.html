Labbox OVF forced-air drying oven, designed for drying, heating, thermal treatment, and other laboratory applications requiring controlled and uniform temperature conditions. The equipment operates within a temperature range of 40 °C to 300 °C, featuring microprocessor-based temperature control and forced-air circulation to improve thermal uniformity throughout the chamber.

The stainless-steel inner chamber and adjustable shelves facilitate equipment use and cleaning, while the double-glazed viewing door allows samples to be monitored without opening the chamber. The system also includes over-temperature protection, sensor failure alarm, and timer functions.

Main Applications
  • Sample and reagent drying;
  • Material warming;
  • Thermal treatment;

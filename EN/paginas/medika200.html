Fiocchetti MEDIKA 200 TOUCH professional refrigerator, with a capacity of 221 L, designed for the storage of vaccines, reagents, biological samples, cultures, analytical materials, and other temperature-sensitive products. The equipment operates within a temperature range of +2 °C to +15 °C, providing stable and controlled storage conditions for laboratory, clinical, and research applications.

The system features a tempered glass door, fan-assisted cooling, and electronic control via a 7-inch touchscreen, with a temperature resolution of 0.1 °C and integrated temperature and alarm data logging. Real-time monitoring, alarm systems, and connectivity options enable continuous monitoring of storage conditions and facilitate data management.

Main Applications
  • Storage of biological samples;
  • Conservation of reagents;
  • Storage of extracts;
  • Conservation of cultures;
  • Storage of temperature-sensitive materials;

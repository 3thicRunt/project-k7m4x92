TOH Echotrac CV100 Ecosonde, designed for high-resolution echo sounder measurements in aquatic environments. The instrument uses advanced echo sounder technology to provide detailed bathymetric and underwater visibility information, making it suitable for laboratory and field applications involving rivers, estuaries, coastal waters, and other aquatic environments. It allows for precise measurements of water depth, using a single or dual channel echo sounder.

Main Applications
  • Bathymetry;
Main Advantages:
  • Precise bathymetric measurements using acoustic waves;
  • Multiple time varied gain (TVG) curves (10, 20, 30, and 40 log);
  • DSP digitizer with manual filter control;
  • Manual or auto scale changes (phasing);
  • Calibration menu with controls for transducer draft and index plus sound velocity and bar depth controls;

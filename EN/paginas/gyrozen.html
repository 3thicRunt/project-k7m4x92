Gyrozen GZ-2236R refrigerated centrifuge, designed for sample separation by centrifugation in laboratory and research applications. The equipment allows the processing of different sample volumes and container formats, featuring rotors compatible with 250 mL bottles and 50 mL Falcon tubes, making it suitable for the concentration, separation, and preparation of biological and environmental samples.

The GZ-2236R features speed and temperature control, allowing centrifugation conditions to be adjusted according to the characteristics of the sample and the experimental procedure. The refrigeration system maintains samples at low temperatures during centrifugation, reducing the risk of compound degradation or changes in the properties of temperature-sensitive samples. The equipment is particularly suitable for applications requiring the processing of large sample volumes or multiple samples simultaneously.

Website: JFE Advantech Co., Ltd.

JFE Advantech INFINITY-WH AWH-USB, water-pressure-based wave height meter designed for monitoring wave height and water-level variations in aquatic environments. The instrument uses a semiconductor pressure sensor to measure hydrostatic pressure and determine water depth, enabling high-resolution acquisition of wave-related data.

The system supports continuous and burst measurement modes, with sampling intervals from 0.1 to 600 seconds, making it suitable for the characterization of both short- and long-period waves. Data are stored on a waterproof microSD card and can be configured and transferred through a USB connection.
